::: intro
Your instructor is a working practitioner in cyber defense. Nothing meets live,
so that time goes to the board, your reports, and students who have gone quiet.
:::

::: section | What gets graded by a person
An autograder scores most of your work, and you can run it yourself before the
deadline.

Your instructor grades the parts a machine cannot judge: whether your finding is
accurate, and whether your fix is adequate.
:::

::: steps | How to send a message that gets answered
1. **Ask whether another student needs this answer.** If yes, post it on the Q
   and A board instead.
2. **Put the course name in the subject line.**
3. **Say what you tried and what happened.** Paste the command and the exact
   error text. Do not describe it from memory.
4. **Wait two business days, then send it again.** Messages do go missing, and
   nobody minds a second try.
:::

::: section | What your instructor expects from you
1. **Start each week early.** Setup problems take a day to solve, not an hour.
2. **Ask before you are stuck for four days.** Staying stuck and silent is what
   ends courses.
3. **Read the Rules of Engagement and follow them.** That is the one page where
   a mistake has consequences outside the gradebook.
:::
