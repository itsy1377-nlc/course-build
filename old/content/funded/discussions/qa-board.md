::: intro
Ask anything here about the course, your notebook, or an error you cannot get
past. It is not graded, it stays open all term, and your instructor answers
within one business day.
:::

::: callout stop | Never post your gateway token
Your token is not just a password. It spends money. Every call made with it is
billed to the class budget and recorded under your name.

That makes a token in a post worse than a password in a post. Anyone who reads
it can spend your budget, and the record will say you did.

- Never paste your token into a post, a reply, or a code block.
- Never post a picture of your notebook with the token on screen. Read every
  line of an image before you attach it.
- If a token does reach this board, message your instructor the same minute. It
  gets turned off and you get a new one.

Nobody is in trouble for reporting a leak quickly. Clause 3 of the Rules of
Engagement covers tokens.
:::

::: steps | How to ask so it takes one reply, not four days
1. **What you were trying to do.** One sentence.
2. **Which notebook and which cell.** Name both.
3. **What you expected to happen.** Many questions here turn out to be a
   correct result that looked wrong.
4. **What happened instead.** The exact error text, copied. Do not retype it
   from memory and do not photograph your screen.
5. **What the check cell says.** Run it first, then paste its last line.

Title the post with the problem. "Check cell says the token is missing" is
findable. "Help please" is not.
:::

::: section | How to answer somebody else
Answering is the fastest way to learn this material.

- **Point, do not paste.** Name the cell and the idea. Do not hand over your
  code for graded work.
- **Say how you know.** "I hit this too, and my token had not loaded" helps
  more than a bare command.
- **Be kind about basics.** Many people here had never opened a notebook.

You may share how something works. You may not share your code, your results,
or your answers.
:::
