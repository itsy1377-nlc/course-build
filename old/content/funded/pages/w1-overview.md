::: intro
The first part of this week takes about twenty minutes. You change one line of
working code, add one line above it, and watch a model decide by itself to call
your function. The rest of the week you break it on purpose.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week you will be able to:

1. Describe what your agent did on one run, citing a field in its record.
2. Say what each of six deliberate breaks changed, and name the field that shows it.
3. Mark each of your tools reversible or not, and give the reason.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
You may work with three things this term, and nothing else:

1. The practice target this course gives you.
2. The agent you build in your own notebook.
3. A model reached through the school gateway.

Never a commercial AI service. Never a system somebody else owns. Read the Rules
of Engagement page in Start Here before you run anything. The gate quiz checks
that you have, and it also tells you what the gateway records.
:::

Students new to programming commonly take two to three times as long on the
coding. That puts a realistic week between about 10 and 20 hours. If any one
part passes two hours, stop and post on the Questions and Answers board. Being
stuck is normal and it is not a reason to fall behind.
