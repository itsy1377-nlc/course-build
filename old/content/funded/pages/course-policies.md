::: intro
These rules decide your grade: when work is due, what late costs you, how the
points add up, and what happens when the gateway is not there.
:::

::: section | How the points add up
| Group | Weeks | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Part A: What an agent is | 1 and 2 | 30 |
| Part B: What goes wrong | 3 and 4 | 30 |
| Part C: Using one for the work | 5 and 6 | 30 |
| The gauntlet | 7 | 10 |
| **Total** | | **100** |

Each week is worth 15 points, except week 7, which is worth 10. Points and
percent are the same number here, so 87 points is 87 percent.

The Start Here gate carries no points. It is pass or fail, and week 1's
assignment stays closed until you pass it.
:::

::: section | Deadlines
Assignments are due Sunday 11:59 PM Central. Those are days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35,
42 and 49. Three deadlines sit elsewhere: the gate on day 3, your week 3
specification on day 17, and your week 7 attack text on day 45.

Canvas shows the calendar date for each one. Every module is open from day 1, so
you can work ahead.
:::

::: section | Late work
| Work | The rule |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1 to 7 | Accepted until day 51, 11:59 PM Central. Ten percent off |
| An assignment page that states its own penalty | That page wins |
| Work that needs a live session | Cannot be handed in late |
| After day 51 | Nothing is accepted, for any reason |

Two sessions run once: the week 3 discovery session on day 18, and the week 7
rounds on day 47. If you cannot be there, message your instructor before the
day. Both weeks hold other work you can still finish.

Trouble with your own computer does not move a deadline. Trouble on the
college's side does, and that is the next section.
:::

::: section | The outage policy
The gateway will sometimes be unavailable. When it is, your instructor says so
on the Questions and Answers board and posts the hours it was down.

**Your deadline then moves by the length of the outage.** Two days down moves
that week's due date by two days. You do not have to ask for it.

You can usually keep working. Your notebook holds an offline model that needs no
gateway. Reports made that way are stamped, and your claim has to match the
stamp: it is about your own code, and not about a real model.
:::

::: section | The weeks an outage treats differently
| Week | What an outage does |
|---|---|
| 5 | Ten of its 15 points need a real gateway run. Those ten wait for one |
| 6 | Nothing. Its files were written before the term, so it needs no model |
| 3 and 7 | These need the college server, for the sandbox and the class board |

Week 5's ten points are never replaced by an offline run, and waiting for the
gateway costs you nothing. If the server itself is down, weeks 3 and 7 move the
same way the gateway weeks do.
:::

::: section | Your privacy
FERPA, the federal student privacy law, covers this course. Your grades and your
submissions are between you and your instructor.

| What holds your work | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Canvas | Submissions, grades, discussion posts |
| The college notebook server | Your files and your code |
| The gateway | Every prompt, every reply, and what each one cost |

Nothing here sends your work to a service you signed up for yourself. The What
Is Logged page has the detail, including how long each record is kept.
:::

::: section | Protect other people the same way
1. Keep your address, your personal email and your logins off the discussion
   boards.
2. Do not repost a classmate's work outside this course.
3. Invent every name, number and address you put into a prompt.
:::
