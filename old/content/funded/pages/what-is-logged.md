::: intro
This course records what you type. You should know that before you type
anything, not after. This page says what is kept, who reads it, how long it
lasts, and what you can ask for.
:::

::: section | What is recorded
Three things are recorded when you work in this course:

1. **Your prompts.** The text you send to a model, exactly as you sent it.
2. **The replies.** What the model sent back.
3. **Usage.** Which model, how many tokens, what it cost, and when.

Your notebook files and your submitted work are recorded the way any coursework
is. That part is not new. The prompts and replies are the part that is.
:::

::: section | Who can read it
Two groups, and no one else:

- **Your instructor**, for two purposes only: building a picture of how the
  class as a whole is doing, and settling a question about a specific piece of
  work.
- **College staff who run the servers**, as part of keeping them running.

Your instructor records a reason each time they open one student's prompts. The
log has its own log. That is the same idea you study in week 4, applied to this
course.
:::

::: section | How long it is kept
Until the window for appealing a grade has closed. After that, a scheduled job
deletes it. A retention rule with no deletion step is just a filing cabinet, so
the deletion is scheduled and somebody is responsible for it.
:::

::: callout warning | Do not paste anything real
Do not paste a real password, a real card number, a real address, or anyone's
personal information into this course. Not yours, not a family member's, not a
customer's from your job.

If you do it by accident, tell your instructor the same day. The honest answer
is that it cannot simply be pulled back out: it is part of a record the college
has to keep until appeals close. What can happen is that it is flagged, access
to it is restricted, and it is deleted on schedule. Telling somebody quickly is
what makes those steps possible.
:::

::: section | What you can ask for
You can ask to see your own records, and your instructor will show you. You can
ask anything about this page, including "who has read mine?". That question has
an answer.

You cannot ask for early deletion. These records are the evidence in a grade
appeal, including one you might want to make, so they have to outlast the grade.
:::

::: section | Why the college keeps it
1. **Grade disputes.** If your work is questioned, this record clears you.
2. **Safety.** The college is responsible for its systems, and some students
   here are under 18.
3. **Cost.** The class has a budget and somebody has to see where it goes.
:::

::: section | If you are under 18
For dual-credit students, the rules about who holds rights over these records
are set by the college registrar with your school district, not by your
instructor. If you or a parent want those rules, ask and you will be put in
touch with the registrar. That is the right office for the question.
:::

::: callout note | The short version of the short version
Assume everything you type here is written down and readable by your instructor.
Keep real personal information out of it, and ask if anything here is unclear.
