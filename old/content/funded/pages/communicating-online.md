::: intro
Most of this course is you, a notebook and a message board. This page says where
to write, and how to write so somebody can help you.
:::

::: section | Three places, three jobs
| Where | What it is for | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| Questions and Answers board | Anything about the work | The whole class |
| Canvas Inbox | Grades, absence, anything private | Your instructor |
| The class board, weeks 3 and 7 | Your submitted work, under a handle | The whole class |

Ask on the board first. Your question is rarely yours alone, and one answer
there reaches everybody.
:::

::: section | How to write a post somebody can answer
1. Name the week and the cell you were running.
2. Say what you expected, then what happened.
3. Paste the error text as text, not as a photograph of a screen.
4. Say what you have already tried.

Four lines like that are usually enough. A post saying only that something does
not work cannot be answered, and waiting for a reply costs you a day.
:::

::: section | Writing about a classmate's work
Weeks 3 and 7 put your work in front of the class. Week 3 hands you three tools
with no names on them. Week 7 runs everyone's attacks against everyone's
defences.

- Write about the tool or the attack, never about the person.
- Do not try to work out who wrote what before the reveal.
- Use the handle shown on the board, not a real name.
- Keep anything you would not read aloud in class out of your submission.
:::

::: section | What never goes in a message
- Your gateway token, or anybody else's.
- A real password, card number or address.
- Anything private about a real person, including you.
- A working attack aimed at a system outside this course.

What you type into the notebook, the board and the gateway is recorded under
school control. The What Is Logged page says who may read it.
:::

::: callout note | Who is in the room
Your classmates range from students still in high school to adults returning
after years at work. Write the way you would at a job: plain, direct and
patient. Nobody here is expected to know this material already.
:::
