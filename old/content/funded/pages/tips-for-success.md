::: intro
What separates a comfortable week from a hard one here is not programming
skill. It is a handful of habits, and every one of them is on this page.
:::

::: steps | Do this now
1. Open the week's overview on its first day and read the time table.
2. Read the content page before you touch the notebook.
3. Start your runs early. Thirty trials take real time to finish.
4. Write up while the run is fresh, not on Sunday night.
5. Submit before 11:59 PM, with the files the assignment asks for.
:::

::: section | Two hours stuck is a signal
It is not a measure of whether you belong here. Every overview page says so, and
it is meant literally.

Post on the Questions and Answers board with the exact error text. Copy it as
text. Do not retype it from memory, and do not photograph your screen.
:::

::: section | Spend the budget on purpose
Your gateway budget is small and it has to last the whole term. Every call
spends a little of it.

Run the check cell before you call the model. It reads your setup and names the
fault in one sentence, and it costs nothing. Use the reset cell when a break
gets away from you, rather than running again and hoping.
:::

::: section | Claim only what your evidence carries
This course grades small claims with a citation under each one. Name the file,
the line and the field.

A test that found nothing is a result, and it is paid the same. Write what it
rules out, then stop.
:::

::: section | Keep everything
Your notebook folder grows all term. Week 5 uses the tool records you wrote in
week 1. Week 7 turns on the guards you filled in during week 5.

Nothing you build gets thrown away, so name your files so you can find them
again.
:::

::: callout tip | If the week gets away from you
Message your instructor through the Canvas Inbox before the deadline, not after
it. Say which part you are on. A problem raised early has options that the same
problem raised late does not.
:::
