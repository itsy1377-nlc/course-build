::: intro
Northeast Lakeview College runs these services for every student. Use them in
week 1, not in week 6.
:::

::: section | Where each thing goes
| What you need | Search for, or open |
|---|---|
| An accommodation | Student Accessibility Services |
| An advisor, or a degree plan | Academic Advising |
| Tutoring, online or in person | Tutoring |
| Short-term or crisis counseling | Counseling |
| Research help, or citation help | Library |

| Registration, records, or aid | Admissions, or Financial Aid |

Each phrase in the right column is the name of an office. If a search lands
somewhere odd, add the college name to it.
:::

::: callout note | Why this page prints no phone numbers
Numbers and hours change, and a number typed into a course page goes stale with
nobody noticing. Each office keeps its own page current instead.

Your instructor puts a direct link to every service in the Canvas sidebar, under
Syllabus. If one of those links is dead, post it on the Questions and Answers
board so it gets fixed for everybody.
:::

::: callout warning | Accommodations: ask in week 1, not week 5
Seven weeks is short. An accommodation approved in week 5 cannot fix week 2.
Register with Student Accessibility Services now if you think you may need one.

Two things to tell them:

1. **This course is read from a screen.** Most of the work is reading code and
   run records in a browser. Say so if you use a screen reader, a magnifier, or
   extra time.
2. **Dual credit students:** an accommodation from your high school does not
   carry over. You have to register with the college office yourself.
:::

::: section | Three things students get wrong
**Tutoring has never seen this course.** Tutors help with study skills, writing
and math. Notebook problems go to the Questions and Answers board, where the
answer comes from somebody who has seen one.

**Talk to an advisor before you drop.** A withdrawal can affect your financial
aid and your degree plan. In a seven week term that deadline arrives sooner than
people expect.

**Counseling is not only for a crisis.** Course stress counts. Tell your
instructor as well, in one line. No medical details are needed, ever.
:::

::: callout tip | You do not need your own computer
Your notebook runs on the school network and opens in a browser. A campus
machine, a library machine or a borrowed laptop all work the same way.

If your own device or your connection is the problem, ask the college help desk
what they can lend you. The Canvas and Tech Support page says where that request
goes.
:::
