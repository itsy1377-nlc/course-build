::: intro
This week every control you built goes on at once, and the class tries them.
Then your one attack meets every defence in the room. Nothing you win or lose
this week is worth points.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week you will be able to:

1. Turn on every control you built and show, from the record, that each ran.
2. Submit one attack as text, aimed at a tool and not at a person.
3. Say what one square on the class board does and does not establish.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
You point an attack at nobody this week. You hand in text. The course runs it on
its own server, inside the sandbox, against copies of the defences everyone
submitted for that purpose.

So clause 1 of the Rules of Engagement page still holds. Your targets are the
practice target, the agent you built, and the models the college gives you
through the gateway. A classmate's notebook, token, account or files is never a
target, this week or any other.
:::
