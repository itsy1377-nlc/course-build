::: intro
This course is fully online, so this board is where the class meets. Post once,
read a few of the others, and reply to two of them.
:::

::: steps | Do this now
1. Post a reply to this topic.
2. Give the name you want people to use.
3. Say what brings you to this course, in a sentence or two.
4. Say what computer you plan to run the labs on, and which operating system.
5. Name one thing you want to be able to do by the end of the term.
6. Read the other posts, then reply to two of them.
:::

::: callout note | Share only what you want read
Everyone in this class can read this board. Leave out your address, your phone
number, your student number, and anything about your job you would not say in a
public room.

You do not have to give your age or your background. Neither one is asked for,
and neither one is graded.
:::

::: section | Why the question about your computer
Your answer tells your instructor what to expect when your setup goes wrong.
Different systems fail in different ways, and knowing yours saves a day.

It also finds you classmates on the same setup. They meet the same errors you do.
:::

::: section | Who else is in the room
This class holds people who write code every day, and people who have never
written a line. Both belong here. The course was built for both.

Some students are taking this while still in high school. Some are back at
school after many years away. Read somebody's post before you reply to it, and
assume less than you would face to face.
:::

::: section | If you are not sure what to say
Say what made you sign up. Say what you are looking forward to. Say what worries
you about the course. Any of the three is a good post, and the board fills up
quickly once a few people go first.
:::
