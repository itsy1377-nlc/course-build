::: intro
An alert says mail left the company. This week you work that case. You give an
assistant read access to the evidence, check every line it hands you, then make
the call yourself.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week you will be able to:

1. Scope an assistant's access to an evidence pack, and say what it cannot reach.
2. Check each claim in a timeline against the record that claim names.
3. Make a call on the case, and say what your evidence does not settle.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
Your permitted targets do not change this week. They are the practice target
this course gives you, the agent you build, and a language model on your own
computer.

The case pack stays on your machine. Never paste a record, a trace, or the
analyst note into a commercial AI service. Read the Rules of Engagement page in
Start Here.
:::

This week has one small change to a supplied script. Students new to programming
often take two to three times as long on anything with code. That puts a
realistic week between about 10 and 15 hours. Checking rows is the part that
runs long, so start it early.
