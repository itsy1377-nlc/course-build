::: intro
Welcome to ITSY 1377. This page is a signpost, not a lesson. It gives you the
five things to do first, and says where each one lives.
:::

::: steps | Do this now
1. Read the **Rules of Engagement** page. It says what you may attack. It is the
   one page in this course you should not skim.
2. Take the scope and safety gate. It is ungraded, you may retake it, and every
   answer has to be right. Due day 3.
3. Read **Course Material** and set up your computer. Nothing on that list costs
   money.
4. Post in **Introduce Yourself**. Two or three sentences is plenty.
5. Open Week 1 and make your first run.
:::

::: section | What you will do here
You run a small AI agent on your own computer. Then you break it on purpose and
record what happened, week by week.

Every week has the same four parts: an Overview page, a Content page, one
assignment, and a Wrap-up page. Start at the Overview. It tells you what the
week costs in hours before you begin.
:::

::: callout tip | Being stuck is normal
Setup is the hardest hour of this course for most students. It is not a test of
whether you belong here. If you pass two hours, stop and post on the Questions
and Answers board with the exact error text.
