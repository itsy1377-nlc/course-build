::: intro
This page holds the rules that decide your grade: when work is due, what happens
when it is late, how the four groups add up, and what stays private. Read it
once now, then come back when you need one answer.
:::

::: section | Deadlines
Each week runs seven days. Its assignment is due on the last day of that week,
at 11:59 PM Central time.

| Due | What |
|---|---|
| Day 3 | The scope and safety gate |
| Day 7 | Week 1 assignment |
| Day 14 | Week 2 assignment |
| Day 21 | Week 3 assignment |
| Day 28 | Week 4 assignment |
| Day 35 | Week 5 assignment |
| Day 42 | Week 6 assignment |
| Day 49 | Week 7 assignment, the evidence file, and the open book exam |
:::

::: section | Late work
The weekly assignments for weeks 1 to 7 are accepted late, up to **day 51 at
11:59 PM**. After that Canvas closes them. If a late penalty applies, your
instructor posts it in Canvas.

The evidence file and the open book exam are due day 49 and cannot be late.
Final grades are posted straight after, so there is no room to hold them open.

Submit what you have. A partial answer on time is worth more than a perfect one
that never arrives.
:::

::: section | How your grade is worked out
| Group | What is in it | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Part A: What an agent is | Weeks 1 and 2 | 20 |
| Part B: What goes wrong | Weeks 3, 4, and 5 | 30 |
| Parts B and C: Controls and the work | Weeks 6 and 7 | 20 |
| The evidence file | The report, 20, and the open book exam, 10 | 30 |
| **Total** | | **100** |

Each weekly assignment is worth 10 points. The course is 100 points in total, so
your point total is also your percentage.
:::

::: section | If a system goes down
Nothing in this course depends on an outside service, so almost nothing can fail
and stop your work. Canvas is the exception.

If Canvas or a college system is down when work is due, your deadline moves by
the length of the outage. Message your instructor through the Canvas Inbox once
service returns, and say what you saw and when.

Your own laptop, your own internet, and a slow model are not outages. Late work
through day 51 is what covers those.
:::

::: section | Two checks that open the rest of the course
1. The **scope and safety gate** in Start Here. Week 1's lab stays closed until
   you pass it. There is no limit on attempts.
2. The **scope answer inside the week 1 assignment**. It is pass or fail, and
   week 3 stays closed until it passes.

Neither one carries points. Both are required.
:::

::: section | Your work stays on your computer
Everything you run happens on your own machine. No prompt, no record of a run,
and no result is sent to any service.

Your instructor sees what you submit in Canvas, and nothing else. Your grades
and your submitted work are protected student records, so your instructor
discusses them with you by Canvas Inbox, never on a discussion board.
:::

::: callout warning | Keep real data out
Do not put real personal information into the practice target or into anything
you submit. Not your own, not a family member's, not a customer's from your job.
The practice data is invented, and it stays that way.
:::
