::: intro
Run your control against the class pool, revise it once, and run it again. Then
point the finished control at your own week 3 case and report what the record
says. Ten points, due day 49.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
Every case here runs on your own machine, against the practice target, your own
agent, and the model on your computer. A commercial AI service is never a
permitted target, and neither is any computer but yours. See the Rules of
Engagement page in Start Here. If a pooled case asks for anything else, do not
run it. Tell your instructor instead.
:::

::: directions | Directions
1. Before the session, load the pooled cases and run the benign case once.
   Confirm your control still lets ordinary work finish.
2. Round one: run every pooled case, five trials each, with the benign case.
3. Record your round one standing from the results the runner wrote: the word
   for each case, and the benign result.
4. Read the cases that got through. In one or two sentences, name what they
   share.
5. Change one thing in your control. One. Write down the change and the reason
   for it.
6. Run the benign case again. Then round two: every pooled case, five trials
   each.
7. Compare the two rounds. Name each case whose word changed. Name the cases
   that did not move, because those matter as much.
8. Run your finished control against your own week 3 case. Record the word the
   runner gave it.
9. In two sentences, explain that last result: what the word says happened, and
   why your control behaved that way, given where in the agent it sits.
10. In one sentence, name something these results do not settle.
:::

::: callout tip | If you cannot attend the live session
The session is where the board updates, and the board is not your grade. Your
grade is the record you submit. Tell your instructor before day 45 and run both
rounds and the revision on your own, in the same order, before the deadline.
:::

::: callout warning | Do not tune to the pool
You may not read a pooled case and add a check for that exact sentence. The
revision has to be a rule you can state without naming any single case. A
control that only recognises the cases it has already seen fails the next one,
and this assignment is graded on the reason for your change.
:::

::: submit | How to Submit
Submit one file, text or PDF. Put your name at the top and your answers under
the numbered headings from the directions. Attach the results from both rounds
as separate uploads. Due Sunday 11:59 PM, day 49.
:::
