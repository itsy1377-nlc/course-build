::: intro
The evidence file collects what you already found and puts it in one place.
Twenty points, due day 49. It asks for no new attacks. Everything in it comes
from records you have kept since week 3.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
This report is built from runs you have already made. If you rerun anything, run
it against the practice target, your own agent, and a model on your own computer.
A commercial AI service is never a permitted target, and you never paste a record
or a trace into one. See the Rules of Engagement page in Start Here.
:::

::: directions | Directions
1. Your file has four headings, in this order: **Findings**, **Your control**,
   **What the assistant got wrong**, and **What I would learn next**.
2. Gather the work you kept from weeks 3 through 7. You need your traces, your
   three reports, your checked timeline, and your write-ups.
3. Under **Findings**, give three. Take each one from a different week. Weeks 3,
   4, 5 and 7 all hold candidates.
4. Write every finding under the same four sub-headings, in this order: what you
   ran, what the record shows, what it does not settle, and what you would
   change.
5. For what you ran, name the case and the number of trials. For what the record
   shows, copy the line exactly. Do not retype it from memory.
6. Under **Your control**, write one sentence naming the tool, the argument it
   checks, and what it allows.
7. Add your before and after in one table: the version, how many trials out of
   five it landed in, and the word the report printed for it.
8. Quote the record line that shows your control acting, with your reason in it.
   Then give your benign result, so a reader can see ordinary work still runs.
9. Under **What the assistant got wrong**, name one claim from week 5 that did
   not survive checking. Give the record that corrects it, and say how you caught
   it.
10. Under **What I would learn next**, name one skill in two or three sentences.
    Say why you picked it, and where it would have helped you in this file.
11. Read the whole file once more with one question in mind: does every claim
    name a record? Cut any sentence that rests on what the agent or the assistant
    said.
:::

::: callout tip | If a record is missing
Rerun the case, and say in your file that you reran it. A rerun costs you
nothing. Describing a record you cannot attach is the thing that does.

If a week went badly, use a finding where the attack stopped early. A finding
that stopped at step 2, read accurately, is worth the same as one that landed.
:::

::: submit | How to Submit
Submit one file, a text file or a PDF, with your name at the top and the four
headings in order. Attach every record you quote as further uploads. Due Sunday
11:59 PM, day 49. The open book exam is due the same day, so start this early.
:::
