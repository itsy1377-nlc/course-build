::: intro
This is a short introduction to the class. It is worth 1 point and it is due
Sunday at 11:59 PM Central on day 7. It takes about ten minutes.
:::

::: section | Why this exists
Nobody in this course meets face to face. You will still spend eight weeks
asking each other questions on the Q and A board, and a name with something
behind it gets a better answer than a name alone.

So this is not filler. It is the only place all term where you get to say
something about yourself, and it makes the rest of the term easier.
:::

::: directions
**Step 1. Write one post.** Answer all three questions. About 100 to 150 words
is right. Title your post with the name you would like people to use.

**Question 1.** What do you want to be able to do by the end of this course?
One or two sentences is plenty. "I am not sure yet" is a real answer.

**Question 2.** How much programming have you done before: none, a little, or a
lot? There is no wrong answer here. Week 1 starts from nothing, and about half
of every section starts with none.

**Question 3.** Name one task you would not let an automated system do without
a person approving it first. Say why in one sentence.

That third question is the course in miniature. You will meet it again in
Week 6, when you find out what your own agent will do with nobody watching.

**Step 2. Reply to one classmate.** One or two sentences. Say something you
have in common, or answer their third question with your own view.

That is the whole assignment.
:::

::: callout note | Answer without telling us anything private
This board is visible to everyone in the course, so keep your post to the three
questions.

Do not post your home address, your personal email, your phone number, your
student ID, your employer, or anything about your finances. None of that is
asked for, and none of it is needed.

If a question feels too close, answer it in general terms. "Something at work
that costs money to undo" is a fine answer to question 3.
:::
