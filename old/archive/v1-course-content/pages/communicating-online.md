::: intro
In a fully online course, what you write is all anyone sees of you. Write so a
hiring manager could read the thread.
:::

::: callout stop | Never post a credential
Your API key is a credential. So is a password or a token.

1. Never paste a key into a post, a message, or a chat.
2. Never post a screenshot with a key visible. Crop it, or retype the part you
   need.
3. Never paste a key into a model prompt.

If a key reaches somewhere public, act the same minute. Turn it off, make a new
one, and tell your instructor. Staying quiet causes the harm.
:::

::: section | How to write here
- **Skip abbreviations.** Not everyone knows them.
- **Do not type in capitals.** It reads as shouting.
- **Proofread before you post.** You cannot take a post back.
- **Say the helpful thing first**, then the correction.
- **Ask a question** instead of stating that someone is wrong.
- **Be patient with mistakes.** Everyone makes them, including your instructor.

Nobody can see your face. A reply that felt efficient can read as angry, and
sarcasm rarely survives.
:::

::: section | Disagree about the claim, not the person
You will disagree about technical things, and that is how the field works.

Bring evidence: a result line, a file, a line number. An argument here is settled
by what the system did, not by who sounded more certain.

Do not brag about breaking something. Never describe an attack outside your own
lab, even as a joke. If a thread gets heated, stop replying and message your
instructor.
:::

::: section | Privacy on the boards
Keep your own address, personal email, and logins off the boards. Never repost a
classmate's work or details. Course Policies has the FERPA rule.

Everything in the lab is invented. Never bring a real ticket or a real customer
into a post.
:::

::: section | Zoom, for office hours
1. Sign in with your first and last name.
2. Check your background and your microphone.
3. Mute yourself when you are not speaking.
4. Never share a meeting link outside the course.

Bring your exact error text.
:::
