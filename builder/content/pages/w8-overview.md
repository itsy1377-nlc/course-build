::: intro
Week 8 is the final exam and nothing else. There's no reading, no lab, no discussion and no video. The exam sets no new objectives, so this page lists none. It measures the ones weeks 1 through 7 already set.
:::

::: section | What the exam is
|  |  |
|---|---|
| Questions | 40, multiple choice |
| Points | 30, three quarters of a point each |
| Share of your course grade | 20 percent |
| Attempts | 1 |
| Time limit | None |
| Open book | Yes |
| Opens | Day 50, Monday 12:00 AM |
| Due and closes | Day 56, Sunday 11:59 PM |

The window is seven days wide and nothing else is due inside it. You get one attempt, so open the exam when you've a clear hour and three quarters. If your browser or your connection fails part way through, email your instructor: a technical failure is not your attempt.

**Open book here means your own work.** Your notebooks, your export files, the weekly readings and the email files are all allowed. None of them is a shortcut. Every question hands you the facts it turns on, so an open file helps only where you did the work that taught you to read them.

Eleven of the emails on this exam are new. They aren't in the set you worked with, and nobody has run an agent on one. Each question hands you the facts and asks what your agent should report.
:::
