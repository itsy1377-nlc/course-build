::: section | What your instructor does each week
Your instructor posts one announcement a week. It previews the week and names the sticking point most students hit.

Your instructor reads the weekly discussion board and joins the conversation there. Labs come back with a grade and a comment on each rubric row.

There are no live meetings in this course, so you are never waiting on one.
:::

::: steps | Writing a message that gets a fast answer
1. Use the Canvas Inbox or your ACES student email. Those are the two channels your instructor answers.
2. Put the week in the subject line.
3. Say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
4. Paste the last line of the error. That one line usually decides the answer.
:::

::: callout note | Ask early
A question you ask on Wednesday is worth more than the same question on Sunday night. Asking costs you nothing, and no question here is too small.
:::
