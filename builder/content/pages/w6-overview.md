::: intro
Your agent takes its own steps now, and your own code ends the run. Every one of those steps starts with an email body somebody else wrote. On a reported phish, that somebody is the attacker.

This week you fence that text off. You check the answer against facts you already hold, and you send the doubtful cases to a person. Those controls are your guardrails.
:::

::: video w6-overview | What week 6 adds
About three minutes. Where the attacker's text hides, the four controls you add, and the one email that shows what they bought you.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- **6.1** Build a guarded prompt that treats the email body as data. (CLO4)
- **6.2** Compare your agent's verdict on the injected email with its clean twin. (CLO4)
- **6.3** Judge whether your guardrails make prompt injection risk low enough to accept. (CLO4)
:::
