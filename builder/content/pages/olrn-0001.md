::: intro
The college asks students who are new to online courses to take a short, free course called OLRN-0001. This page says who takes it and how to sign up.
:::

::: keyfacts
|  |  |
|---|---|
| Cost | None. It is a no-fee, 0-credit mini-course |
| How it runs | 100 percent online, self-paced |
| How long | About 1 to 2 hours |
| When | By the first week of class |
| Passing | 80 percent or higher on the post-test |
| Already done it | Nothing to do. Your instructor can see that |
:::

::: section | Who has to take it
> If you're new to online/hybrid courses, or if you'll be taking EDUC-1300 at Northeast Lakeview College, you're required to enroll in a free Orientation to Online Learning (OLRN-0001) mini-course. However, if you've completed OLRN, you do NOT have to retake it.

OLRN-0001 teaches the habits that make an online course work. It also covers the tools you use. You can start it and stop it as often as you need.

Your instructor checks for proof that you passed during the first week. Nothing in this course is graded on it.
:::

::: reading | Signing up for OLRN-0001
The OLRN student page lists the course numbers you can sign up for in ACES. Open it, pick one, and sign up the way you would for any class. Ask yourself one thing while you read: which start date fits the week you've?
