::: intro
Welcome to ITSY 1377, Agentic AI and Security Operations. This is an eight week online course. You build a working AI agent that sorts reported phishing email, one capability a week.
:::

::: section | How this course is delivered
This eight week course is fully online. It has no meeting day and no meeting time. Every module opens on day one. You work through the week on your own schedule.

You turn work in through Canvas. You write and run code on the college's hosted notebook server. You install nothing.
:::

::: section | Your instructor
Reach your instructor through the Canvas Inbox or your ACES student email. You get an answer in 24 to 48 hours, not counting weekends and holidays. Lab grades and feedback arrive within one week of the due date.

The page **Meet your instructor** has the rest.
:::

::: steps | Getting started
1. Read the syllabus. It's the page **Syllabus, policies and grades**. It says what the course covers, what it asks of you, and how your grade is figured.
2. Work down the **Orientation Module** in order. It says how a week runs and where each thing lives.
3. Introduce yourself on the **Introduce yourself** board, so your instructor and your classmates know who you are.
4. Open **Student Resources**. It covers how Canvas works and what the college can do for you.
5. Take the **Week 0 self-assessment**, in Module 0. Ten questions, about ten minutes. Your score says whether to work week 0 or skip it.
:::

::: callout note | Getting help
Canvas help is always available from the **Help** button in the left menu.

The page **Getting help**, in the Orientation Module, names the right person for each kind of problem. The page **Your computer and the notebook server**, in Student Resources, has the help desk numbers.
:::
