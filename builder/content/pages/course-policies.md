::: section | What this course is
You build one working agent, a week at a time. An agent is a program that sends text to a model, reads the answer, and acts on it. It is a phishing email triage assistant.

It reads a reported email, gathers the facts that matter, and writes a note an analyst can act on.

Seven weeks each add one capability to that agent. Week 8 is the final exam and nothing else.
:::

::: section | What you need before you start
The prerequisite is the college's AI literacy course. It teaches you to write a prompt and judge an AI answer, and it is enforced.

That course teaches no programming. This one asks a few lines of Python every week. Week 0 closes that gap, and it is open from day one. It asks 4 to 6 hours.
:::

::: objectives | Course learning objectives
| Objective | Level | By the end of this course you can |
|---|---|---|
| CLO1 | Apply | Extract the header, authentication, sender, and URL facts an AI model needs to judge a suspicious email. |
| CLO2 | Analyze | Classify a suspicious email as malicious or benign using an AI model, then validate the model's output before trusting it. |
| CLO3 | Create | Build an AI agent that selects and runs triage tools in a bounded loop to reach a verdict. |
| CLO4 | Evaluate | Judge whether an agent's guardrails reduce prompt injection risk to an acceptable level. |
| CLO5 | Apply | Document a triage decision, its indicators, and its recommended action so another analyst can act on it. |
| CLO6 | Evaluate | Measure agent triage accuracy across a batch of emails and recommend where human review stays required. |
:::

::: section | How your grade is figured
Four groups make up your grade. Each is worth a set percent, whatever its points total.

| Group | Percent | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| Labs | 60 | Seven labs, one a week, 100 points each |
| Final exam | 20 | One exam in week 8, 40 questions |
| Discussions | 12 | Seven discussion boards, 20 points each |
| Self-checks | 8 | Seven short quizzes, 12 points each |

Week 0 and the weekly Python sidebars are not graded. The syllabus acknowledgment is not graded and does not appear in your grades. It is still required.
:::

::: section | Feedback on your labs
You get a grade and written feedback on each lab within one week of its due date.

Send messages through the Canvas Inbox or your ACES student email. You get an answer in 24 to 48 hours, not counting weekends and holidays.
:::

::: callout note | Your token budget
A token is a chunk of text, roughly four characters. It is the unit the AI model is billed in.

Your instructor sets your budget. Every notebook shows a running count.

If you run out, message your instructor through the Canvas Inbox or your ACES student email and ask for a top-up. Running out costs you no points. Waiting for a top-up costs no late penalty.
:::

::: section | Late work and resubmission
Your instructor states the late work and resubmission policy in the first announcement of the term. Read it before your first lab is due.

One thing is settled now. Nothing in this course locks except the final exam, so you can still open and submit any week's work. The exam opens on the Monday of week 8 and closes when it is due.
:::
