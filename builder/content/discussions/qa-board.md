::: intro
Welcome to the question and answer board. This is the place to ask about the course, and to answer each other. Nothing here is graded.
:::

::: directions | What to post here
Post here when an assignment confuses you, when an idea hasn't landed yet, or when you want to say something about the course material to the class.

Your instructor answers questions on this board. So do your classmates. If you know the answer, please jump in.

A question about your own grade goes to your instructor instead, through the Canvas Inbox or your ACES student email.
:::

::: callout tip | Turn on notifications
Click **Subscribe** at the top of this board. Canvas then tells you when someone posts, so an answer reaches you without your checking back.
:::

::: callout warning | Two things to leave out
Never post an API key, or any part of one. An API key is a secret string that proves your code may use the model.

Never post personal details either, your own or anyone else's.
:::

::: submit | How to post
Click **Reply** at the top of this board and type your question. To answer someone, open their post and click **Reply** under it.

Paste the last line of an error message when you've one. That line is usually what somebody needs to help you.
:::
