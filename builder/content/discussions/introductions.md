::: intro
This is where the class meets. Post a short introduction, then read a few others. It's worth 1 point and it doesn't change your grade.
:::

::: directions | What to post
Write four or five sentences. Cover these four things.

1. Your first name, and what you want to be called.
2. Your program, and how far along you're.
3. What you've done with AI tools so far. No experience is a fine answer.
4. One thing you want to be able to do by the end of this course.

Then read two other posts and reply to one of them. A reply is one or two sentences. Say what you've in common, or ask one question.
:::

::: callout note | What to leave out
Post nothing you wouldn't put on a public noticeboard. Every classmate and your instructor can read this board. Leave out your address, your phone number and your student ID.
:::

::: grading | How this is scored
| What you do | Points |
|---|---|
| One post covering the four things, plus one reply | 1 |
| Anything less | 0 |

The Orientation group is weighted at 0 percent, so this point doesn't change your course grade. Post anyway. A course where people know each other is an easier place to ask a question.
:::

::: submit | How to submit
Click **Reply** at the top of this board and write your post. Then open a classmate's post and click **Reply** under it.

Post by Sunday of week 1, 11:59 PM Central.
:::
