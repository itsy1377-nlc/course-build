::: callout note | Why this schedule uses day numbers instead of dates
The course uses day numbers because two of the eight Flex sessions have a
different shape.

Six sessions start on a Monday and run 55 days. Both Spring Flex I sessions
start on a Tuesday and run 61 days, with a week of Spring Break inside. One
printed calendar would be wrong for those two. Day numbers are right for all
eight.

Canvas shows you the real date for every deadline in your own session. Open the
Calendar in the left menu, or look at the due date on any assignment.
:::

::: section | The weekly rhythm
Weeks run Monday through Sunday. Work is due **Sunday at 11:59 PM Central**
unless a page says otherwise.

That gives seven weekly deadlines: days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, and 49. After
those comes a six-day final block with no Sunday in it.

The last day to withdraw falls somewhere near day 39 to 43. Check Canvas for the
exact date, and talk to an advisor before you use it.
:::

::: section | The final block, day by day
The final block runs Monday through Saturday. There is no Sunday in it, so every
deadline here is a weekday deadline.

| Day | What happens | Due at 11:59 PM Central |
|---|---|---|
| 50, Monday | Exam review released. No new content. The late window opens on Project 2 Part A at 90 percent credit | |
| 51, Tuesday | Backlog day. Hard close on all late work from Weeks 1 through 7 | Any remaining late work |
| 52, Wednesday | The final exam window opens | Final Part B: Security Assessment Report, 120 |
| 53, Thursday | The exam window stays open | |
| 54, Friday | The exam window closes | Final Part A: Comprehensive Exam, 120. Final Part C: Three-Minute Walkthrough, 30 |
| 55, Saturday | Nothing is due. Your instructor grades and closes the term | |

**The exam window is Wednesday through Friday**, days 52 to 54. It is
asynchronous, so take it whenever you like inside that window.

The exam is open book. AI use is permitted and must be declared. You get one
attempt with a three-hour limit, so set aside a block of time before you start.
:::

::: callout stop | Nothing moves after day 54
The term ends on day 55, and grades are due immediately after. There is no room
on the far side of the exam.

That means no incompletes, no make-up work, and no late submissions in the final
block. Read the Course Policies page for the protections that do exist, and use
them while they are still available.
:::
