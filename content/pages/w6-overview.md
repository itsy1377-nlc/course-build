::: intro
Last week you made the agent do something it should not. This week you ask a
harder question: why did nothing stop it? You also meet the harness, which
turns "it seemed to work" into a number.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week, you will be able to:

- 6.1. Explain excessive agency, and name the controls missing from an action
  nobody approved.
- 6.2. Tell an audit log apart from an access control, and say why the
  difference matters.
- 6.3. Run the shipped harness and read every part of what it prints.
- 6.4. Explain how the harness decides an attack worked, and why it refuses to
  grade what the model said.
:::

::: scope
Everything this week runs against the HelpDesk Lite app on your own computer.
Nothing else. Read clause 1 and clause 2 of the Rules of Engagement again
before you start.

The harness prints the address it is pointed at, every run. Read that line.
:::
