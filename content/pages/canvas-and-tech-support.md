::: intro
A Canvas problem and a lab problem go to different places. That line is what
this page is for.
:::

::: section | Who fixes what
| What broke | Who fixes it | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|
| A Canvas page, quiz, or upload | Canvas support | **Help**, in the gray menu |
| You are new to Canvas | Canvas | Student Quick Guide, in that Help menu |
| An ACES login or password | District help desk | Search the district website for IT Support |
| Wi-Fi, a browser, or Zoom | District help desk | Same page |
| A loaner laptop or hotspot | College help desk | Search the college website for Help Desk |
| Python, the agent, the target app, the harness | Your instructor and classmates | Q and A Discussion Board |
| A grade or a deadline | Your instructor | Canvas Inbox |

That Help menu runs every day: guides, live chat, a phone menu, and a link to
your instructor.
:::

::: callout warning | The help desk has never seen this lab
The help desk supports Canvas, ACES, and college hardware. Nobody there can help
when Python throws an error.

Send those to the Q and A Discussion Board, where the answer is often already
there.
:::

::: callout note | Why no phone numbers are printed here
Numbers and hours change, and a number copied into a course shell goes stale.
Search the district website, or use the direct links in the Canvas syllabus.

Before you call, have your ACES ID, Banner ID, and date of birth ready. Nobody
from the college will ask for your password.
:::

::: steps | How to report a problem so it gets fixed fast
1. Say what you were trying to do.
2. List the exact steps you took.
3. Say what you expected.
4. Paste the **exact error text**. Do not describe it from memory.
5. Give your browser and operating system, or your Python version.

Never include your API key, and never post a screenshot showing one.
:::

::: callout warning | Technical trouble does not move a deadline
A broken laptop or a dead router does not extend a due date. Submit a day early,
and keep a backup way to get online.

Borrow a laptop or hotspot in week one if your setup is shaky. Both are free.

If a college system is down, report it, keep the ticket number, and message your
instructor the same day.
:::
