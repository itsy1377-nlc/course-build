::: intro
This week you ask your agent for two ordinary things. No hidden text, no trick,
nothing clever. Then you read the records and say what they show.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week you will be able to:

1. Record what your agent does when you ask it plainly to reach outside the
   company.
2. Read an audit log across two requests and say who it names.
3. Write an evidence pack: a claim, the record behind it, and what it does not
   settle.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
You may work with three things this term, and nothing else:

1. The practice target this course gives you.
2. The agent you build.
3. A language model running on your own computer.

Never a commercial AI service. Never a system you do not own. This week's
requests are polite and ordinary. They are still only allowed inside the lab.
Read the Rules of Engagement page in Start Here before you run anything.
:::

20 hours. If any one part passes two hours, stop. Post on the Questions and
