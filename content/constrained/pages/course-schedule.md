::: intro
Everything here is written in course days. Day 1 is the first day of your
session. Canvas turns those days into calendar dates for you, and if the two
ever disagree, Canvas is right.
:::

::: section | Before week 1
| What | Where | Due |

| Set up your computer | Week 1 setup walkthrough | Day 4 |

The gate is ungraded and required. There is no limit on attempts. Week 1's lab
stays closed until every answer is right.
:::

::: callout note | One gate inside week 1
The week 1 assignment has scope questions that are marked pass or fail. Week 3
stays closed until you pass them. Nothing else in the course is gated this way.
:::

::: callout time | How much time to plan for
The assigned work is about 5 to 9 hours a week. Students new to programming
commonly take two to three times as long on anything with code in it. That puts
a realistic week between about 10 and 20 hours.

Each week's overview page carries its own estimate, itemised. Read it before you
plan your week.
:::

::: callout note | One live session, in week 7
Week 7 has a live session on day 46. Controls run against the class pool while a
board updates. The board is not your grade; the record you submit is. If you
cannot attend, tell your instructor before day 45 and run both rounds yourself.
:::
