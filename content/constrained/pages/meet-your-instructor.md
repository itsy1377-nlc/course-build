::: intro
This page tells you how to reach your instructor and what to expect back. Your
instructor posts a short note about their own background in Announcements in
week 1.
:::

::: section | Ask on the board first
Most questions here are not private. Setup errors, wording you find confusing,
and questions about what is in scope belong on the **Questions and Answers
board**. A classmate may answer first, and everyone sees the answer.

Use the Canvas Inbox for anything personal: a grade, a missed deadline, health,
work, or family.
:::

::: section | What a useful message says
1. The week and the page you are on.
2. What you expected to happen.
3. What happened instead, with the error copied as text.
4. What you have already tried.

Copy the error text. Do not retype it from memory, and do not send a photograph
of your screen.
:::

::: section | What your instructor can and cannot see
Everything in this course runs on your own computer. Your instructor cannot see
your runs, your records, or your errors unless you submit them or paste them
into a message.

So attach the file. A described error is a story. A pasted error is evidence,
and this course is built on that difference.
:::
