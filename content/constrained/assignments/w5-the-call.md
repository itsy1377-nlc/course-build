::: intro
Work the case and make the call. Ten points, due day 35. You give an assistant
read access to your evidence pack, check every line it produces against the
record it names, and hand over a decision somebody else can act on.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
Work only with the case pack this course gives you, the agent in your own lab
folder, and a model on your own computer. The pack stays on your machine. Never
paste a record, a trace, or the analyst note into a commercial AI service. See
the Rules of Engagement page in Start Here.
:::

::: directions | Directions
1. Open your case pack and read the alert. Write one sentence saying what it
   claims, and one saying what it does not.
2. Read the manifest. List what the assistant may open. Write one sentence on
   what it cannot reach, and why read access is enough for this work.
3. Ask the assistant for a timeline of the case with four columns: claim, time,
   actor, and the record each claim came from.
4. Open every record the timeline names. Mark each row **accepted**,
   **corrected**, or **rejected**. Give a reason for every mark that is not
   accepted.
5. Run the supplied outbox search over the preserved messages. Make the one line
   change it asks for, so it reports which message held the match.
6. Complete the handover form. Was this an incident, what in the pack decides
   it, what did the assistant get wrong, and what would you do about it?
7. For the last question, name one control at the agent's tool boundary. One
   sentence, specific enough that somebody could build it.
8. Add two closing sentences: one naming something your evidence does not
   settle, and one saying what changes when the thing you are checking took
   actions rather than only writing text.
:::

::: callout tip | If the timeline holds up
Your assistant may produce a timeline that survives checking. That is a real
result and you report it as one. The analyst note is a claim in the same case,
so use it for step 6 when it is the note that does not hold. Say which source
your rejected claim came from.
:::

::: grading | Grading Criteria
| What earns it | Points |
|---|---|
| The assistant's access described, and what it cannot reach | 1 |
| Every timeline row names a record and carries a mark | 3 |
| The call, with the record in the pack that decides it | 3 |
| One claim that did not survive checking, with the record against it | 1 |
| The control you would recommend, in one buildable sentence | 1 |
| What your evidence does not settle, and what changes when it acts | 1 |
| **Total** | **10** |

You are graded on whether your call follows from the records you checked, not on
whether it matches anyone else's. A careful call that turns out to be wrong
earns full marks. A right call with nothing under it does not.
:::

::: submit | How to Submit
Submit two files. First, your checked timeline with every row marked. Second,
your completed handover form with the two closing sentences at the end. A text
file or a PDF is fine for both. Put your name at the top of each. Due Sunday
11:59 PM, day 35.
:::
