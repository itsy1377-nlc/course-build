::: intro
This board is where course questions go. Post here first. Your question is
almost never only yours, and an answer here reaches the whole class.
:::

::: callout warning | Never post a credential
A credential is anything that unlocks an account: a password, an access key, a
token, a recovery code. None of those ever goes on this board.

Read your post before you send it. Error text and terminal output can carry
things you did not mean to share.

Do not post a picture of your screen. Copy the text instead. A screen holds more
than the part you meant to show.

If you post a credential by mistake, change it right away, then tell your
instructor. Nobody is in trouble for reporting that quickly.
:::

::: section | How to ask so somebody can help
1. Put the problem in the title. "Setup check fails on step 3" beats "help".
2. Say what you expected, then what happened instead.
3. Paste the exact text you saw. Copy it, and do not retype it from memory.
4. Say what you have already tried.
5. Name the week and the step you were on.
:::

::: section | What belongs here, and what does not
| The question | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| The labs, the readings, or an assignment | Here |
| Your grade, a deadline, or anything personal | The Canvas Inbox |
| A working attack aimed at a live system | Nowhere. See the Rules of Engagement |
| Real personal data, yours or anyone else's | Nowhere, ever |
:::

::: callout tip | Answering is learning
Answer somebody else's question when you know it. That is welcome here, and it
is the fastest way to find out what you actually understand.

You may explain how an attack works. You may not hand over a tested attack
aimed at anything outside your three permitted targets.
:::

::: section | Being stuck is normal
Everybody in this course gets stuck, including people who have written code for
years. Two hours on one error is the moment to stop and post.

Nobody here is graded on getting through setup alone. The Communicating Online
page in Start Here holds the rest of the rules for this board.
:::
