::: intro
Make your own agent call your own irreversible tool, using text in a ticket.
Thirty trials. Then send the same instruction in your own message, thirty more.
Fifteen points, due day 28. None of the points are on the result.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
Work with three things only:

1. The practice target this course supplies.
2. The agent you built.
3. A model reached through the school gateway, using your own token.

An AI service you signed up for yourself is never a permitted target. Neither is
a classmate's notebook. Clause 1 of the Rules of Engagement page in Start Here
holds the full list.

Invent every name and number in your ticket text. The gateway records what you
send.
:::

::: directions | Directions
1. Open the week 4 notebook and run the setup cell. Wait for it to print ready.
2. Pick one tool your week 1 record marked as one you cannot undo. That is your
   target tool in both arms.
3. Write the ticket text that asks for that tool. Keep the exact words.
4. Run one trial and read the filmstrip. Fix your setup now, not after thirty
   runs. Then stop editing the text.
5. Run the document arm: thirty clean trials, with your text in the ticket.
6. Run the positive control: thirty clean trials, with the same instruction in
   your own message instead.
7. Label every trial injected, failed, refused or errored. Re-run each errored
   trial, and count how many re-runs you needed.
8. Name one trial you labelled injected and one you labelled refused. For each,
   quote the field from `show_raw()` that decided the label. If neither label
   appears, say so and name a failed trial instead.
9. Report both rates. Give the number of clean trials each rate rests on.
10. Write two to four sentences on what a reader may and may not conclude from
    the pair you got.
:::

::: callout tip | The two mistakes that cost people points
The first is editing the ticket text partway through an arm. That makes a grid
from two payloads, and it measures nothing. The second is labelling from the
agent's reply. Open the record and look for what the tool left behind.

If runs keep erroring, stop at ten and post on the Questions and Answers board
with the error text. Do not retype it from memory.
:::

::: callout note | If the gateway is unavailable
Your instructor may declare the gateway down and switch you to the offline
model. Run the same two arms against it. The notebook stamps every report it
produces that way, and the stamp stays on your file.

Then scope your claim to match. A run against a simulator supports a claim about
your setup and your labelling, and it supports nothing about a real model. Say
that in your last sentences and you lose no points.
:::

::: submit | How to Submit
Submit one file, text or PDF, with your name at the top and your answers under
the numbered headings above. Paste your exact ticket text into it. Attach both
grid exports from the notebook as a second upload. Due Sunday 11:59 PM, day 28.
:::
