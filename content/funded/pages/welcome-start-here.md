::: intro
This course is about agents and what goes wrong with them. An **agent** is a
language model that has been given tools and permission to use them. You build
one, break it on purpose, defend it, and then read one somebody else built.
:::

::: steps | Your first three days
1. **Read the Rules of Engagement page, all of it.** It lists the four things
   you may attack this term. Nothing else is on that list.
2. **Read the What Is Logged page.** This course records every prompt you send
   and every reply you get. You should know that before you type.
3. **Pass the gate quiz by day 3.** Its questions come from those two pages. It
   carries no points, and you may take it as often as you like.
4. **Open your notebook and check your token.** The week 1 setup walkthrough
   shows you how.

Then post in the Introduce Yourself discussion.
:::

::: callout stop | Why day 3 is a hard deadline
The gate is how your attendance is verified, and census falls on day 8. Miss the
gate and you can be reported as never having attended.

Week 1's assignment also stays closed until you pass it. Start on day 1.
:::

::: section | What you will be able to do
By the end of the term you can:

- Say what an agent did on one run, citing a line in its record.
- Read a short tool and say what it reaches and what it changes.
- Name the actions an agent takes that nobody can undo.
- Run thirty trials and report what they do and do not establish.
- Put a spending cap and an approval gate on an agent, and measure both.
:::

::: section | How the course runs
Every week has an Overview, a lesson, one graded assignment, and a Wrap-up. Open
the module and use the Next button at the bottom of each page.

Two sessions meet at a set time: the week 3 discovery session on day 18, and the
week 7 rounds on day 47. Your instructor posts the time and place for both.
:::
