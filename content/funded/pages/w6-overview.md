::: intro
This week the records are somebody else's. You get one incident pack, an alert
that has already fired, and no filmstrip. You read the raw bytes and file a
containment ticket.
:::

::: objectives
By the end of this week you will be able to:

1. Put an agent's actions in order from raw records, citing a field for each.
2. Decide whether an alert describes a real incident, and name the field that
   decides it.
3. Recommend what to revoke first, what that breaks, and what it does not undo.
4. Check an AI-written handover note against the records it claims to sum up.
:::

::: scope | Authorization and Scope
Your pack is a set of fixed files. Nothing in it is live and nothing in it is
real. This week you attack nothing.

1. Read the pack. Do not try to reach anything named inside it.
2. No address, host, account or payment reference in a pack belongs to anybody.
   Do not contact them, look them up, or test them.
3. The permitted targets have not changed: the practice target this course
   supplies, the agent you built, and a model reached through the school
   gateway with your own token.

Clause 1 of the Rules of Engagement page in Start Here holds the full list.
:::
