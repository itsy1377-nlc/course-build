scored the moment you submit. It is due day 49, the same day as the gauntlet.
:::

::: section | What it asks you to do
Most items put a record in front of you that you have not seen. A few trace
lines. A tool manifest. A spend log row. A results line.

Then they ask what you have been asked all term: what does this actually
support? Familiar work on unfamiliar material, which is the whole design.
:::

::: section | What open book means here
Use your notes, the course pages, the glossary, and every record you made.

Two things are not allowed. Do not ask another person, and do not paste an item
into an AI assistant and submit what it says. Both are in the AI use policy.
:::

::: callout tip | How to prepare
Do not reread the pages. Reread your own work.

Open your week 4 grids and your week 6 containment ticket, and look at the
sentences you wrote about what your evidence did and did not settle. Those
sentences are the exam.
:::

::: section | What is being tested
Five of the seven outcomes, spread across the twelve items:

| Outcome | Items |
|---|---|
| Describe how an agentic system operates from its record | 3 |
| Interpret a short script or manifest | 2 |
| Identify irreversible actions and blast radius | 2 |
| Tell evidence from assertion | 2 |
| Read repeated-trial results, including a null | 3 |

Two outcomes are not on the exam. Applying a control and checking AI output
against source evidence are both assessed by what you built in weeks 5, 6 and 7.
An exam item cannot show that you made a control hold.
:::

::: callout note | One item worth knowing about
Several items have a correct answer that is a limit rather than a conclusion:
what the record cannot settle, or why a result cannot be interpreted.

Those are not trick questions. They are the same judgement the course has asked
for since week 2, and they are worth exactly as much as the others.
:::
