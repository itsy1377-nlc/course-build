::: intro
A Canvas problem, a login problem and a notebook problem go to three different
places. Sending yours to the right one is what this page is for.
:::

::: section | Who fixes what
| What broke | Who fixes it | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|
| A Canvas page, quiz, or upload | Canvas support | The Help menu in Canvas |
| A college login or password | District help desk | Search the district website for IT Support |
| Wi-Fi, a browser, or a device to borrow | College help desk | Search the college website for Help Desk |
| The notebook server or the gateway | Your instructor | Questions and Answers board |
| Your code, your agent, your records | Your instructor and classmates | Questions and Answers board |
| A grade or a deadline | Your instructor | Canvas Inbox |
:::

::: callout warning | The help desk has never seen this course
The help desk supports Canvas, logins and college hardware. Nobody there can
help when your agent throws an error or your token stops working.

Send those to the Questions and Answers board. The answer is often posted
already.
:::

::: section | When the notebook or the gateway is down
This is not your fault, and it is not graded as though it were.

1. Check the Questions and Answers board. Somebody has often posted already.
2. If nobody has, post what you saw and roughly when you saw it.
3. Work on the parts that do not need it, such as your write-up.

Your instructor declares the outage and moves the deadline by the length of it.
Week 5 is the one to watch. Ten of its points need a real gateway run, so those
points wait for the gateway rather than being replaced.
:::

::: callout note | An outage is a college system, not your laptop
Your own dead laptop or router is a different problem. The notebook opens on any
campus machine, so start early and you have somewhere to go.

Submit a day early where you can. That habit costs nothing and it has saved a
lot of grades.
:::

::: steps | How to report a problem so it gets fixed
1. Say what you were trying to do.
2. List the steps you took, in order.
3. Say what you expected to happen.
4. Paste the exact error text. Do not retype it from memory.
5. Name your browser and the time it happened.
:::

::: callout stop | Never post your token
Your gateway token is money and it is tied to your name. Keep it out of posts,
messages, screenshots, and any file you hand to a classmate.

If you think somebody has seen it, tell your instructor the same day. A token
can be replaced. Clause 3 of the Rules of Engagement page covers this.
:::
